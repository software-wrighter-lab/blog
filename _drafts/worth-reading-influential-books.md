## The Psychology of Computer Programming

Gerald M. Weinberg (1971; a silver anniversary edition in 1998). Programming as human behavior: programmers working alone and in teams, and egoless programming.

## The Mythical Man-Month

Frederick P. Brooks Jr. (1975; the 1995 anniversary edition adds *No Silver Bullet*). Brooks's law, that adding people to a late software project makes it later, and the line between essential and accidental complexity that [sw-checklist](/2026/04/27/sw-checklist-ratchet-ai-coding-agents/) leaned on.

## A Pattern Language

Christopher Alexander, Sara Ishikawa and Murray Silverstein, with Max Jacobson, Ingrid Fiksdahl-King and Shlomo Angel (1977). 253 patterns for towns, buildings and rooms, each a problem and its solution, and the source of the idea that *Design Patterns* carried into software.

## Threaded Interpretive Languages

R. G. Loeliger, *Threaded Interpretive Languages: Their Design and Implementation* (BYTE Books, 1981). How to build a Forth-like language from an inner interpreter up, on a small machine. ## To Mock a Mockingbird

Raymond Smullyan (1985). Combinatory logic as puzzles about an enchanted forest of birds. Its birds have flown through this blog: the [Sage](/2026/09/21/rabbit-hole-sage-y-combinator/), the [Phoenix](/2026/10/19/rabbit-hole-phoenix-combinator/), the [birds in APLSV](/2026/09/24/tbt-aplsv-birds-tttml/), and [in APL, Lisp and Forth](/2026/10/29/why-i-use-apl-lisp-forth/).
