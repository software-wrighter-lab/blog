## Gödel, Escher, Bach

Douglas Hofstadter, *Gödel, Escher, Bach: An Eternal Golden Braid* (1979). Formal systems, recursion and self-reference, told through Gödel's incompleteness theorem, Escher's drawings and Bach's canons, with dialogues between the chapters. Its "strange loops" are systems that refer to themselves, like a [Forth that rebuilds itself](/2026/10/24/embedded-forth-from-forth/) or the [Sage bird](/2026/09/21/rabbit-hole-sage-y-combinator/).
