## Next: the IBM 1130

Work is planned to complete my IBM 1130 emulator and run, if possible, actual APL, Forth and Lisp code written for the 1130. It will also draw on my 3D models of the machine, [console-3D](https://github.com/softwarewrighter/console-3D) and [s1130-3D](https://github.com/softwarewrighter/s1130-3D), and earlier 1130 work, to animate the console, keypunch, printer, disk and the rest. The goal is to update the Computer History Museum on my research campus.
