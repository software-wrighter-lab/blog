## The Design of Everyday Things

Donald A. Norman (1988, first as *The Psychology of Everyday Things*; revised 2013). Why doors, stoves and switches confuse people, and what a design owes its users: visible affordances, clear mappings, feedback, and forgiving errors.

Steve Krug, *Don't Make Me Think: A Common Sense Approach to Web Usability* (2000; later editions 2005 and 2014). A web page should be self-evident; people scan rather than read, and a little usability testing beats a lot of argument.
