Scott Rosenberg, *Dreaming in Code: Two Dozen Programmers, Three Years, 4,732 Bugs, and One Quest for Transcendent Software* (2007). Three years with Mitch Kapor's Open Source Applications Foundation as its team tries to build Chandler, a personal information manager, and why the software takes so much longer than anyone planned.
